## 7. Exact Future Pilot Procedure

Do not execute this procedure in this task.

1. Select 20-50 Items from `SYNTHETIC`, `PUBLIC`, or explicitly
   `AUTHORIZED_EXTERNAL` data only.
2. Cover at least two domains, such as electronic engineering and computer
   science/design thinking.
3. Fix one Provider safe id, one model safe id, one prompt version, and one
   output schema version.
4. Require request cap, retry cap, concurrency cap, versioned pricing, monetary
   cap, and cost-before-socket preflight.
5. Send only English term plus minimal safe English context; do not send source
   UID, file path, private course text, user identity, or raw document content.
6. Allow abstention and classify unresolved/noise separately from failed output.
7. Persist only evaluation artifact rows; do not create approved
   `ConceptAlignmentCard`, do not mutate Formal Runs, and do not write Provider
   output as document evidence.
8. Stop fail-closed on missing credential, stale or missing pricing, exhausted
   budget, unsafe endpoint, non-JSON response, schema violation, unsafe output,
   or private-source denial.

## 8. Stop Conditions Before Formal Workflow Integration

- Do not enable real Provider in Formal Workflow.
- Do not route `POST /api/document-alignment-runs` to external Provider.
- Do not use private course PDFs or chunks without a separate auditable
  external-processing permission.
- Do not treat Provider-generated proposal as document evidence.
- Do not auto-approve cards.
- Do not rely on model self-reported confidence as platform confidence.
- Do not proceed without budget, pricing, credential redaction, and endpoint
  allowlist.
