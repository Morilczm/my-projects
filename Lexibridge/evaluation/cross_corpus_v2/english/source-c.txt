Energy and Interactions

Power is the rate at which work is performed. Work is energy transferred when a force acts through displacement.

Gravitational potential energy depends on position in a gravitational field. Kinetic energy is associated with motion.

Mechanical energy combines kinetic and potential contributions. Conservation of mechanical energy applies when nonconservative transfer is absent.

Force can alter motion through an interaction. Weight is a gravitational force and should not be confused with mass. Magnetic flux density is an additional field quantity outside this benchmark.
