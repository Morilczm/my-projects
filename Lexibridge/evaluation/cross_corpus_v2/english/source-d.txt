Foundations of Translational Mechanics

Impulse equals force accumulated over a time interval. Momentum is mass multiplied by velocity, while linear momentum is a more explicit name used in some contexts.

Inertia is resistance to a change of motion. Mass measures an object's inertial matter content and is distinct from weight.

Acceleration is the time rate of change of velocity. Velocity includes both speed and direction.

Displacement is the directed change from an initial position to a final position. Speed, distance, and path length are neighboring but different ideas.
