Electricity Seminar Handout

Capacitance is the stored charge divided by the potential difference. Electric current and current density are useful distractors but are not the same quantity.

An electric field assigns force per unit positive test charge at a location. Electric field strength is often discussed nearby.

Electric charge is a property responsible for electrical interaction. Potential difference compares electric potential at two locations.

Electric potential is potential energy per unit charge. Electric potential energy instead belongs to a charged object in a configuration.
