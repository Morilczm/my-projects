Rotational Motion Notes

Angular velocity gives the time rate of change of angular position. Angular acceleration describes how angular velocity itself changes.

Torque measures the turning action of a force about an axis. Moment of inertia characterizes rotational resistance and is a useful nearby concept.

Angular momentum describes rotational motion and depends on rotational inertia and angular velocity. Conservation of momentum states that total momentum remains fixed for an isolated system.

Centripetal force points inward during curved motion. Centripetal acceleration is a related kinematic quantity, while uniform circular motion keeps speed constant along a circular path.
