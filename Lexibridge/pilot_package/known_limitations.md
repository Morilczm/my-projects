# Known Limitations

- Local pilot-ready, not production-ready.
- SQLite and local uploads are used in local mode.
- Mock payment and mock email are not real services.
- Local worker is not a production queue.
- Formula OCR may be unavailable unless a provider is configured.
- Local hash embedding and local JSON vector index are demo retrieval components.
- Teacher review is still required for risky cards.
- Source authorization depends on teacher and pilot owner review.
- Demo success does not prove real-course full accuracy.
