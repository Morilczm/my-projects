# Student Manual

## What You Can Do

- Join or select a course.
- Search English terminology.
- Read Chinese terminology and explanations.
- Check English and Chinese evidence.
- View formula evidence when available.
- Favorite terms.
- Mark terms as mastered or not mastered.
- Submit feedback.
- Upload personal study materials.
- Export review material when your plan allows it.

## Terminology Status

`Teacher Approved`: a teacher has reviewed the card.

`Auto Approved`: the system passed evidence and confidence gates. You can still submit feedback.

`Needs Review`: a teacher still needs to review the card.

`Needs Evidence`: evidence is missing or weak.

`Formula Evidence Missing`: a formula region was detected but formula OCR evidence is incomplete.

`Unverified Translation`: translation suggestion exists, but it is not fully verified by evidence.

## How To Use Cards

1. Search the English term from your course.
2. Read the Chinese term and concept explanation.
3. Check whether the card is teacher approved or still pending.
4. Read the evidence source instead of memorizing the translation only.
5. Favorite difficult terms.
6. Mark mastered terms after review.

Prioritize Teacher Approved cards. Use pending or evidence-needed cards as references only.

## Personal Uploads

Personal materials are private to your account. They are not added to the course public knowledge base and are not searchable by other students. Teachers do not use your personal uploads as course public evidence by default.

## Feedback

Submit feedback when:

- Translation seems wrong.
- Evidence is unrelated.
- Explanation is unclear.
- A term is missing.
- Formula recognition seems wrong.
- You find another issue.

Feedback goes to teachers or the pilot team. It does not automatically edit the terminology card.

## Boundaries

LexiBridge AI is not an exam answer generator and not a general chatbot. It is designed to help you understand course terminology with traceable evidence.
