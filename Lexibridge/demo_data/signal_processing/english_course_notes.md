# SP101 Signal Processing Basics - English Course Notes

Fourier Transform converts a time-domain signal into a frequency-domain representation.

Convolution combines two signals to produce a third signal and is often written as y(t) = ∫ x(τ)h(t-τ)dτ.

Angular Frequency is measured in radians per second and describes the rate of phase change.

Wavelength is the spatial period of a wave.

Time-domain Signal represents how a signal changes over time.

Frequency-domain Representation describes signal content in terms of frequency components.

Sampling Theorem states that a band-limited signal can be reconstructed from samples taken above twice its highest frequency.

Impulse Response describes the output of a system when the input is an impulse.

Transfer Function represents the relationship between system input and output in a transform domain.

Laplace Transform converts a time-domain function into a complex frequency-domain function.

Bandwidth measures the range of frequencies occupied by a signal or supported by a system.

Modulation varies a carrier signal to transmit information.

Carrier Frequency is the central frequency used to carry a modulated signal.

Signal-to-noise Ratio compares desired signal power with noise power.

Phase Shift describes a displacement of a waveform in phase.

Amplitude describes the magnitude or strength of a signal.

Frequency Response describes how a system responds to different frequencies.

Z-transform converts discrete-time signals into a complex frequency-domain representation.

Low-pass Filter passes low frequencies and attenuates high frequencies.

High-pass Filter passes high frequencies and attenuates low frequencies.

Fourier Transform formula sample: X(f) = ∫ x(t)e^{-j2πft}dt.
